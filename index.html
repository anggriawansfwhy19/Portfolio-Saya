About Me

Fresh graduate from Singaperbangsa Karawang University with skills in Android (Java & Kotlin), UI/UX, and endto-end system development. Delivered real projects including a mobile app for Seduh Space Coffee, data analysis for Konstitusi Coffee, a company website for Komando, and an AWS-based game. Proven impact by developing an internal LMS using Microsoft Power Apps integrated with SharePoint, successfully implemented as a platform to support training and recruitment processes at PT Denso Indonesia. Contributed directly by interviewing operator candidates and creating multimedia training materials (video, animation, design) to improve learning delivery. Further strengthened technical skills through Bangkit Academy by building a smart ordering application using the Naive Bayes algorithm. Known as adaptable, collaborative, and detail-oriented, with a focus on delivering efficient, data-driven, and user-centered solutions.
